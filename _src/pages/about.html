About the journal

AI-Driven Energy Research (AIDER) is an international, peer-reviewed, diamond open-access journal. It publishes original research and review articles in which artificial intelligence is a core method for understanding, designing or operating energy systems. The journal was founded in 2026; its founding editorial board is drawn from the University of Oxford, Imperial College London, Kyoto University, Tsinghua University, Delft University of Technology, Harvard University, the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology and China Three Gorges Renewables.

AIDER publishes its articles together with the code and data needed to check and build on them. Reproducibility is part of the editorial assessment, not an afterthought.

Aims and scope

The journal considers work at the intersection of artificial intelligence and energy, where AI is central to the contribution. Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • machine learning for computational fluid dynamics, turbulence and heat transfer;
  • AI-driven power-grid optimisation, operation and planning;
  • reinforcement learning and control for energy systems;
  • generative and data-driven models for energy materials, catalysis and batteries;
  • physics-informed neural networks and surrogate models for energy applications;
  • forecasting for wind, solar and other renewable resources;
  • AI for energy demand, including data centres and electrified industry;
  • automated and AI-assisted research workflows in energy science.

Article types. Research articles and review articles. Proposals for perspectives or special collections may be sent to the editorial office.
