Join the editorial board or reviewer pool

AI-Driven Energy Research relies on researchers who give their time to assess manuscripts and guide the journal. We welcome expressions of interest from researchers at all career stages working on artificial intelligence and energy systems.

Reviewers

Reviewers assess manuscripts within their expertise, following the instructions for reviewers. Invitations state the expected time; a report is normally requested within three weeks. You may also be asked to answer a single, specific question about one section of a manuscript, which takes much less time than a full review. Reviewers may decline any invitation.

To join the reviewer pool, email the editorial office with:

  • your name, position and affiliation;
  • your ORCID identifier or a link to your publications;
  • three to six keywords describing your expertise.

Editorial board

Members of the editorial board handle manuscripts as editors, help recruit reviewers and authors, and take part in decisions about the journal's policies at regular board meetings. The time commitment is typically a few hours per month. Board meetings and their records are published on the news page.

To express interest in joining the board, please write to the editorial office describing your research area and how you would like to contribute.
