About the journal

AI-Driven Energy Research (AIDER) is a peer-reviewed, diamond open-access journal that publishes original research and review articles at the intersection of artificial intelligence and energy systems. The journal covers machine learning for fluid dynamics and energy conversion, power-grid optimisation and control, materials discovery, forecasting for renewable energy, and related computational methods. Articles are free to read and free to publish, and authors are expected to make the code and data behind their results available. Read more about the journal.

Call for papers

The editors invite submissions of original research articles and review articles on all aspects of artificial intelligence applied to energy systems. There are no article processing charges. Please consult the instructions for authors before submitting your manuscript. Topics the editors particularly wish to see are listed under research priorities.
