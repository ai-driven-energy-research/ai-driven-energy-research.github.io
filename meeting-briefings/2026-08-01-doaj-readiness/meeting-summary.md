## DOAJ discussion

AIDER now has an ISSN and five fully published papers. The meeting agreed that credibility must come from a demonstrably independent, quality-controlled publishing process rather than from publication count alone.

The group discussed the need to:

- use at least two independent reviewers for each research article;
- separate editorial and reviewer roles;
- recruit qualified external reviewers;
- maintain transparent review evidence and reproducibility checks; and
- build a publication record that meets DOAJ’s eligibility and endogeny requirements.

The five existing papers remain published, but they do not by themselves demonstrate the independent external-review record needed for a DOAJ application.

## Partnership proposal

Jianou proposed exploring collaboration with the Journal of the Global Power and Propulsion Society, or JGPPS. He reported a positive preliminary discussion with his supervisor, Prof. Budimir Rosic, who is listed on the journal’s editorial board.

The possible collaboration would combine AIDER’s GitHub-native, AI-assisted submission and reproducibility workflow with experienced human editorial and reviewer input. Possible areas include workflow mentorship, reviewer-community learning, a joint reproducibility activity and agent-readable research demonstrations.

Participants reacted positively, but the transcript does not contain a complete, unambiguous formal ballot of every eligible attendee. The proposal is therefore carried to the third meetup as a bounded motion. A positive conversation is not an institutional endorsement, reviewer commitment, indexing transfer or publication agreement.

## Experimental AI track proposal

Feng Wei proposed using credible AI-paper tracks and conferences to test AIDER’s methods, learn from external review and promote the project. The event shown during the meeting was the DAI 2026 AI Paper Track at City University of Hong Kong.
