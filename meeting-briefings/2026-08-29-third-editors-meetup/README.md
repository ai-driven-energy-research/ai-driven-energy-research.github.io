# Third Editors’ Meetup — Actions, Partnerships and Experiments

Source and published briefing for AIDER’s third editors’ online meetup:

- Saturday, 29 August 2026
- 9:00–9:30 pm Beijing time, UTC+8
- Meeting link to be circulated separately

Published PDF: https://ai-driven-energy-research.github.io/aider-third-editors-meetup-pre-read-2026-08-29.pdf

## Purpose

The meeting will:

1. ratify the record of the 1 August meeting;
2. review evidence of progress on the seven proposals passed at that meeting;
3. vote on bounded motions concerning JGPPS outreach, future experimental AI tracks, and a possible workshop or hackathon;
4. check progress against the DOAJ evidence plan; and
5. hear new proposals to be documented and voted on at the following meetup.

The source document distinguishes formal decisions from positive but informal discussion. It also records that DAI 2026’s submission deadline is 10 August 2026, before this meeting, so the 29 August ballot cannot authorise a new on-time DAI submission.
