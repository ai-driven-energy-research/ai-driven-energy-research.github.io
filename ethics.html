Publishing ethics and journal policies

AI-Driven Energy Research follows the Core Practices of the Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE) as guidance for its editorial policies. The policies below apply to authors, reviewers and editors.

Originality and prior publication

Submissions must be original work that has not been published and is not under consideration elsewhere. Preprints (for example on arXiv or SSRN) do not count as prior publication. Text, data or figures reused from the authors' earlier work must be cited.

Authorship

All listed authors must have made a substantial contribution to the work and approved the submitted version. Author contributions should be stated, preferably using the CRediT taxonomy. AI tools cannot be authors.

Research and data integrity

  • Data must not be fabricated, falsified or selectively reported.
  • Processing steps applied to data should be described, and raw data and transformation scripts provided where possible.
  • Image manipulation that changes the meaning of the data is not permitted.

Conflicts of interest

Authors must disclose financial and personal relationships that could be seen to influence their work. Reviewers must decline invitations where they have a conflict. Editors do not handle manuscripts that they authored or that come from their own research group; such manuscripts are assigned to another member of the board and the editor is excluded from the decision.

Use of AI tools

By authors

AI tools may be used in research and writing. Their use must be disclosed in the manuscript's Use of AI tools statement, giving the tool, version, purpose and extent, and how outputs were checked. Authors are responsible for the accuracy and originality of all content. Undisclosed use found during review leads to a request to correct the disclosure; deliberate concealment leads to rejection, or to a correction notice after publication.

By reviewers

Reviewers may use AI tools to assist their assessment, must disclose this in their report and remain responsible for its content. See instructions for reviewers.

By the journal

The journal uses automated tools for administrative tasks: extracting the title, authors, abstract and keywords from uploaded manuscripts to pre-fill the submission form, and initial technical checks such as locating cited references and confirming that required statements and files are present. Editorial decisions are made by human editors on the basis of independent peer review. Automated tools do not accept or reject manuscripts.

Open access and copyright

  • Diamond open access: no charges to authors or readers, no embargo.
  • Authors retain copyright. Articles are published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0).
  • Code is normally released under MIT or Apache 2.0, and data under CC0 or CC BY 4.0.
  • Authors may deposit any version of their article in any repository without restriction.

Publication permanence

Published articles receive a permanent DOI through Zenodo and are part of the scholarly record. They cannot be removed. Published articles may be corrected or retracted as described below, but the original version remains accessible.

Corrections and retractions

Corrections. Errors that do not change the conclusions are corrected by a published correction notice and a new version of the article on Zenodo, linked to the original.

Retractions. An article may be retracted in cases of fabrication or falsification, plagiarism, serious undisclosed conflicts of interest, or errors that invalidate the conclusions. A retraction notice explaining the reason is published, the Zenodo record is updated, and the article is marked as retracted on the journal website. The original remains accessible.

Withdrawal before acceptance. Authors may withdraw a manuscript at any time before acceptance by writing to the editorial office.

Complaints and appeals

Authors may appeal an editorial decision by writing to the editorial office with their reasons. Appeals are considered by an editor who was not involved in the original decision. Complaints about the conduct of the journal or its editors should be sent to jianou.jiang@eng.ox.ac.uk and will be acknowledged and investigated.

Privacy

Manuscripts and personal information supplied with a submission are used only for editorial purposes. They are shared with editors and reviewers as needed for peer review, and may be processed by automated tools, including third-party AI services operated on the journal's behalf, solely for the administrative checks described above. They are not used for any other purpose and are not published unless the article is accepted.
